# Nockers Engineering Products

Nockers Engineering Products is a South African supplier of industrial wire, screening media and related engineering materials. The business was established in 1995 and is based in Craighall Park, Johannesburg.

## Official website

For current product information, specifications and enquiries, visit the official Nockers Engineering Products website:

[https://nockers.co.za/](https://nockers.co.za/)

## Product areas

Nockers supplies materials used across industrial, manufacturing, mining, construction and engineering applications. The published product range includes:

- [Spring wire](https://nockers.co.za/products/spring-wire/)
- [Stainless steel wire and rod](https://nockers.co.za/products/stainless-steel-wire/)
- [Fine copper wire](https://nockers.co.za/products/fine-copper-wire/)
- [Resistance and heating wire](https://nockers.co.za/products/resistance-and-heating-wire/)
- Welding wire, alloy wire and aluminium wire
- Cold heading wire, wire products and metal strips
- Industrial screens, demisters and mist eliminators
- Centrifugal and static castings
- Polypropylene gold electrowinning cells

Material selection depends on the required grade, dimensions, finish, tolerance, mechanical properties, packaging and operating environment. Buyers should confirm current specifications and availability directly with Nockers before placing an order.

## Enquiry information

A useful wire or engineering-material enquiry normally includes the application, material or grade, diameter or dimensions, quantity, packaging format, required standards or certification, delivery location and required date. Packaging may include coils, spools, drums, straight lengths or other formats according to the product and supply requirement.
