# What Is Wicket Wire? Uses, Sizes and Packaging Applications

**Wicket wire** is a Π-shaped galvanised wire form used by the packaging industry to hold and dispense blocks of wicketed plastic bags. It helps present bags consistently for machine loading in bakeries and food-processing plants, or for manual dispensing in retail environments.

## How wicket wire works

A typical wicket is manufactured from 2 mm or 3 mm galvanised wire. The two legs, or prongs, pass through a stack of thin plastic bags and keep the block aligned until each bag is removed for filling.

The same basic format supports automated packing of bread and other food products as well as manual packing operations. The steel wicket can be recycled after the bags have been used.

## Common wicket-wire dimensions

Wicket wires are produced in different proportions. Depending on the packaging equipment and bag format, the prongs can be shorter than, longer than, or equal to the body of the wicket.

- 75 × 75 × 75 mm
- 100 × 75 × 100 mm
- 100 × 125 × 100 mm
- 125 × 150 × 125 mm

These are common configurations from the Nockers technical article. Final dimensions should be confirmed against the bag design and packaging machine requirements.
