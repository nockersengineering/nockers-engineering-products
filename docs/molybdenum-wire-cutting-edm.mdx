# Molybdenum Wire Cutting: Technical Advantages and EDM Parameters

[NOCKERS ENGINEERING PRODUCTS](https://nockers.co.za/) explains the technical advantages of molybdenum wire cutting compared with conventional brass or copper wire cutting.

Molybdenum wire cutting machines can offer advantages over conventional brass wire cutting machines. A commonly used molybdenum wire diameter is 0.18 mm, which meets many design requirements for narrow cuts and near-sharp inside corner radii. The 0.18 mm size can provide a useful balance between lifetime, cutting speed, cutting tolerance and surface quality.

Because of its high tensile strength, molybdenum wire can help maintain wall straightness while reducing the wire breakages associated with copper or brass wires.

In applications where copper or zinc contamination of the cut surface must be avoided, copper-based wires may be unsuitable, making molybdenum or tungsten wires relevant alternatives.

## Tensile strength and cutting advantages

The original Nockers article compares approximate tensile strengths as follows:

- Copper wire: 200–250 MPa
- Soft brass wire: approximately 375 MPa
- Hard brass wire: approximately 1180 MPa
- Molybdenum wire: approximately 1725 MPa

The high tensile strength of molybdenum wire provides several potential advantages:

- Reduced wire breakage.
- Reduced elongation of less than 2% during cutting.
- Higher pulling force to support straighter cuts and resist side forces during angular cutting movements.
- Precise straight-side detail with sharp definition.
- The ability to increase cutting speed for higher productivity where the machine and process allow it.

## Temperature and EDM performance

Molybdenum has a high melting temperature of approximately 2625°C, compared with approximately 1085°C for copper and 900–940°C for brass. Molybdenum-wire CNC cutting machines are designed for high working temperatures, allowing conductive materials—including hard and tough materials—to be cut.

The spark craters left on the wire surface are small compared with those left on the workpiece after cutting, which can contribute to longer wire life than softer wires. With an electrical discharge machine correctly designed to flush burnt metal particles from the cut, the metal-removal rate can be high.

## Typical reference parameters

The original article lists these typical molybdenum EDM parameters:

- Worktable size: 350 mm × 600 mm
- Cutting axis: 320 mm (X) × 400 mm (Y) × 400 mm (Z)
- Maximum workpiece weight: 300 kg
- Maximum machining speed: 170 mm/min
- Wire diameter: 0.10–0.20 mm
- Cutting tolerance: ±0.015 mm
- Surface finish: Ra ≤1.2 mm
- Power supply: 380 V, three phase
- Power consumption: 2.5 kW

These values are reference parameters from the original article. Confirm current wire availability, machine specifications and application requirements before procurement.
