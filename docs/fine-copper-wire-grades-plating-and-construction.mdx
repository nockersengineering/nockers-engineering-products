# Fine Copper Wire: Grades, Plating Standards and Strand Constructions

Fine copper wire is used where electrical conductivity, flexibility, controlled dimensions and reliable strand construction matter. The correct specification depends on the conductor material, plating, single-wire diameter and finished construction.

Nockers Engineering Products lists pure copper grades ETP, C101, OFCu and C103, together with copper-alloy options including brass CuZn10–CuZn37, phosphor bronze CuSn5–CuSn8, and nickel silver CuNi12Zn24 and CuNi18Zn20.

## Bare and plated copper wire

Bare copper wire can be supplied to ASTM B1 and ASTM B3 requirements. Where the application calls for a plated surface, the available specifications include:

- Tin-plated copper wire to ASTM B33, in diameters from 1.00 mm to 2.60 mm, with 25 µm tin plating.
- Nickel-plated copper wire to ASTM B355, in diameters from 0.80 mm to 2.60 mm, with 27% nickel by weight.

Plating selection should be confirmed against the operating temperature, joining process, corrosion environment and other requirements of the finished component.

## Strand and multi-wire constructions

Fine copper wire can be supplied in bunched and stranded constructions up to 360 mm². Multi-wire drawing is available from 1.00 mm down to 0.05 mm, with reels supporting up to 24 ends.

These construction choices allow buyers and engineers to balance conductor area, flexibility, handling and downstream processing needs. The final wire or strand specification should always be confirmed against the application and the required standard.
