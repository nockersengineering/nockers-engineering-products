# Düsseldorf Wire Fair – 2024

In April 2024, [Nockers Engineering Products](https://nockers.co.za/) attended the Düsseldorf Wire & Tube Fair in Germany. The event ran from 15 to 19 April and brought together established suppliers and new participants from across the wire and tube industry.

## Building industry relationships

The fair gave the Nockers team an opportunity to meet many existing suppliers, connect with potential new industry partners, strengthen established business relationships, and begin conversations around new ventures.

Watch this space for future developments.

## Useful and creative product displays

Many exhibitors showcased their products in distinctive but practical ways. Highlights included the striking “Copperman” display, the busy show-floor atmosphere, and a functional table display that made the supplier’s products easy to see and understand.
