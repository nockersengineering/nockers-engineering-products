# Why Visit Suppliers?

[NOCKERS ENGINEERING PRODUCTS](https://nockers.co.za/) purchases from many countries around the world, and our main suppliers are wire mills or downstream wire processors.

These companies have catalogues showing a wide range of wires produced, and their sales departments can quote for any of these. However, specific requirements are often only determined by face-to-face discussions with technical personnel and a visit to their works.

Visiting your supplier demonstrates that you are a unique customer rather than a generic one, like a commodity trader who is mainly interested in availability and price. Top suppliers may obtain a better price for products when they can supply to a higher tolerance or with specific quality attributes.

By visiting suppliers, NOCKERS ENGINEERING PRODUCTS has been able to determine the ideal supplier for each specific requirement by ascertaining:

- What materials and size ranges are in regular production and what are on demand, requiring a minimum order quantity or special purchase of raw material.
- What processes are done in-house and whether any are done by off-site service centres.
- How and where raw materials are purchased, stored and used to ensure final product conformance.
- Process inspection control as well as final quality inspection.
- The segregation and handling of out-of-specification materials.
- Different packaging arrangements.
- Alternative or additional products manufactured that we could offer to our customers.
- Whether prices are appropriate to our customer market in terms of cost and expectation.

Often, visiting the works environment of a prospective supplier is the way to determine the likelihood of a company being able to consistently supply the product required.

NOCKERS ENGINEERING PRODUCTS believes that visiting suppliers is essential in establishing a long-term business relationship.
